Questions

A few quiet answers.

Amaya AI is a secure, AI-powered Electronic Health Record platform designed to create a unified digital health profile for every patient. Patients can securely upload prescriptions, laboratory reports, diagnostic images and other medical documents. Authorized healthcare professionals can access structured medical history.

A LangGraph-based multi-agent AI framework analyzes uploaded records, extracts key clinical information, generates intelligent summaries and builds chronological medical timelines. Specialized agents cover OCR, medical extraction, classification, timeline creation and summary generation.

The system uses JWT authentication, role-based access control, consent-based doctor access, end-to-end encrypted document storage and secure activity logging.
